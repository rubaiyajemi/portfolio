## Research Studies

- **"Gentlemen and Savages": Civilization and Its Discontents – Moral Hypocrisy in *Great Expectations* and *Heart of Darkness***
  Research paper exploring moral hypocrisy, imperial ideology, and representations of "civilized" and "savage" identities in Charles Dickens' *Great Expectations* and Joseph Conrad's *Heart of Darkness* through postcolonial theoretical perspectives.

- **English as the Dominant Language of Instruction in Bangladeshi Higher Education**
  Mixed-methods study examining students' linguistic adaptation and perceptions in EMI contexts across Bangladeshi universities.

- **Parental Perceptions of the SSC Examination System in Bangladesh**
  Qualitative research investigating parental perspectives on high-stakes assessment, educational pressure, and fairness in the SSC examination system.

- **Comparative Effectiveness of Microlearning in Urban and Rural Contexts**
  Quasi-experimental study exploring grammar and vocabulary acquisition among young EFL learners through microlearning approaches.

- **Sociolinguistic Features in Bangladeshi TV Advertisements**
  Discourse analysis examining code-switching, gender representation, language use, and visual strategies in Bangladeshi television advertisements.

- **Incidental Vocabulary Learning in Bangladeshi Secondary EFL Learners**
  Qualitative study investigating incidental vocabulary acquisition through movies, social media, and digital gaming among secondary-level learners.
