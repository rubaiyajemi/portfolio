01 · About

A passion for language, learning, and literature.

Rubaiya Akter ELT Professional · Researcher

I am a research-oriented ELT professional dedicated to shaping how English is taught, learned, and assessed in Bangladesh and beyond.

Currently pursuing my MA in English Language Teaching & Applied Linguistics at BRAC University, I focus on the intersection of Generative AI in ELT, English-Medium Instruction (EMI), language assessment, vocabulary acquisition, sociolinguistics, and postcolonial literary studies.

My work spans the classroom and the page — from designing communicative materials for Bengali-medium learners to investigating how moral hypocrisy and imperial ideology shape canonical literature. I aspire to contribute to higher education as a university professor, blending rigorous research with thoughtful, learner-centered pedagogy.

03 · Research

Selected research studies

A portfolio of original investigations spanning applied linguistics, literature, and education policy.

01
Literature · Postcolonial

"Gentlemen and Savages": Civilization and Its Discontents

Moral hypocrisy and imperial ideology in Dickens' Great Expectations and Conrad's Heart of Darkness, examined through postcolonial theoretical perspectives.

02
EMI · Mixed Methods

English as the Dominant Language of Instruction in Bangladeshi Higher Education

Mixed-methods study examining students' linguistic adaptation and perceptions in EMI contexts across Bangladeshi universities.

03
Assessment · Qualitative

Parental Perceptions of the SSC Examination System

Qualitative research on parental perspectives of high-stakes assessment, educational pressure, and fairness in Bangladesh.

04
Microlearning · Quasi-Experimental

Comparative Effectiveness of Microlearning in Urban and Rural Contexts

Quasi-experimental study on grammar and vocabulary acquisition among young EFL learners via microlearning approaches.

05
Sociolinguistics · Discourse

Sociolinguistic Features in Bangladeshi TV Advertisements

Discourse analysis of code-switching, gender representation, language use, and visual strategies in Bangladeshi advertising.

06
Vocabulary · EFL

Incidental Vocabulary Learning in Bangladeshi Secondary EFL Learners

Qualitative study on incidental vocabulary acquisition through movies, social media, and digital gaming.
