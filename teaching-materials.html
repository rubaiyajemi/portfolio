Teaching Strategies

Methods, techniques, and approaches I use in the classroom — from CLT to TBLT to TPR.

01

Communicative Language Teaching (CLT)

Centering authentic communication over rote grammar — using pair work, role plays, and information-gap tasks to make meaning the priority.

02

Task-Based Language Teaching (TBLT)

Designing real-world tasks (planning a trip, writing a letter, conducting interviews) where language emerges from goal-oriented activity.

03

Total Physical Response (TPR)

Pairing language input with physical action to lower the affective filter and build comprehension before production.

04

Microlearning Integration

Breaking content into short, focused units delivered through digital tools — supported by my own research on urban/rural EFL learners.
